## User Stories

| No. | User Story | Linked Requirements | Acceptance Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US1 | As a student whose car is blocked, I want to contact the blocking driver through the app, so that I can request relocation without needing a phone number displayed on their dashboard. | FR5, FR7, FR9 | **1.** When the student submits a relocation request for a blocking vehicle with a matched account, the request is sent to that account.<br><br>**2.** If no account matches the vehicle, the app informs the student that the driver cannot be contacted through the system. |
| US2 | As a student whose car is blocking another vehicle, I want to receive and acknowledge relocation requests, so that I can respond when another student needs to leave. | FR7, FR8 | **1.** A submitted relocation request appears in the associated recipient’s account and identifies the vehicle to be moved.<br><br>**2.** When the recipient acknowledges the request, the requesting student can see the acknowledgement. |
| US3 | As a student parked in the monitored car park, I want to receive an alert when my car may be blocked, so that I can check the situation before returning to my car. | FR2, FR6 | **1.** When the obstruction condition in FR2 is met and the affected car is linked to a student account, that account receives a possible-obstruction alert.<br><br>**2.** A vehicle that leaves the monitored access zone before exceeding the 60-second threshold does not trigger an obstruction alert. |
