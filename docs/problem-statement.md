# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

- Customers who want to organise parties with suitable backdrops and decorations.
- Party decoration businesses that offer backdrop design, decoration, and booking services.
- Independent vendors who provide complementary party services and want to reach potential customers.

## What exactly is going wrong?

- Customers may find it difficult to discover decoration services and backdrop designs that match their preferred themes and budgets.
- Customers may need to browse multiple platforms or contact different vendors separately to arrange their party decorations.
- Party decoration businesses and independent vendors may lack a centralised platform for presenting their services and reaching customers.

## Why does it matter?

- Customers may spend unnecessary time and effort searching for suitable decorations and coordinating bookings.
- Comparing available designs and services across different sources may make party planning less convenient.
- Businesses and vendors may have fewer opportunities to showcase their services to customers looking for party decorations.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)

Customers who want to organise parties may find it time-consuming to search for suitable backdrops, decorations, and related services that match their preferences and budgets. Browsing multiple platforms and coordinating with different vendors can make the planning and booking process less convenient. Meanwhile, party decoration businesses and independent vendors need accessible ways to showcase their services and connect with potential customers. Party Poppers aims to address these challenges by providing a centralised platform where customers can explore ready-made backdrop templates, request customised designs, discover vendor services, and arrange bookings.
